# Demo Voiceover Script

## Full 3-Minute Version

My Words, My Way is a local-first Android app built with Gemma 4.
It is not an AI therapist, not a psychoanalyst, and not an advisor.
It is a private listening tool that helps people preserve their own words.

Most AI reflection apps respond too quickly.
They summarize the user, interpret the user, and often sound like an authority.
For intimate reflection, that can be unsafe.
The model may answer everything, but it cannot solve the problems of a person's life by knowing everything.

This app takes the opposite path.
The user records voice memos and writes notes in their own words.
Users can always create notes and voice reflections.
There is no paywall.

Gemma 4 E2B runs on device through LiteRT-LM.
It extracts important words from the user's notes and memo text.
The model returns structured JSON only.
It does not advise, diagnose, or explain the user.

The Words tab organizes extracted words in a Lacanian-inspired Borromean knot view.
There are object a fragments, personally important words, and what I truly want.
The app does not say, "this is your truth."
It only preserves words and evidence spans.
The user can edit, delete, export, or recalculate everything.

The app is local-first.
Notes, audio, model setup, exports, and calculation logs stay under user control.
Audio playback can continue while the screen is locked.
Gemma calculation shows progress, backend status, and logs, so the user knows what is happening.

The goal is not to make AI know the user.
The goal is to help the user keep their own words private, searchable, and editable.
Most AI assistants speak for the user.
My Words, My Way helps the user keep speaking until their own words become powerful.
